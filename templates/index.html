💡 How to test signs in front of your camera:
• 👋 Hello / Goodbye: Open hand with all 5 fingers spread, wave to camera.
• 🤟 I Love You: Extend Thumb, Index, and Pinky (curl Middle & Ring).
• ✊ Yes: Make a closed fist with all fingers curled and nod.
• 💧 Water: Extend Index, Middle, and Ring (ASL 'W' handshape).
• 🆘 Help: Make a thumbs-up gesture in front of camera.
• 🤏 No: Extend Index & Middle together and pinch down to thumb.
• Notice: Single random fingers (like pointing index or middle) are strictly rejected as "Unrecognized Sign".
